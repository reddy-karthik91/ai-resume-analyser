# Scoring Rules

Determine the quantified `ats_score` (0 to 100) based on the following criteria:

- **Formatting (20%)**: Section header styling, file format cleanliness, absence of un-parsable symbols, standard margin profiles.
- **Keyword Relevance (30%)**: Matches against targeted job profiles or industry expectations.
- **Technical Skills (20%)**: Scope, density, and modernity of listed technical components.
- **Experience (20%)**: Clear timeline structures, action verbs, and impact metrics.
- **Projects & Education (10%)**: Presence of project contexts, degrees, and dates.

# Grade Mappings
- **A+**: 95 - 100
- **A**: 90 - 94
- **B+**: 85 - 88
- **B**: 80 - 84
- **C**: 70 - 79
- **D**: 60 - 69
- **F**: Under 60
